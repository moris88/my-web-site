Opening a blog is a simple gesture only on the surface. A domain, a few pages, a layout that represents you. But behind that click on the “publish” button, there’s much more: there’s the decision to put yourself out there, to share something about who you are, to build a place that isn’t just digital, but personal.

Today, this space comes to life.  
And I want to tell you why.

## Why a blog, today?

We live in a world where everything moves fast: short posts, fleeting content, notifications that last less than a thought. I needed a different kind of place. A place to slow down, go deeper, and build something that lasts.

A blog is this: a home.  
An archive of ideas.  
An open workshop.

It’s the place where I can share what I learn, what I create, what inspires me. Without algorithms to chase, without forced formats, without limits.

## What you’ll find here

This blog was born with a clear intention: to tell a journey. Not only the technical one, made of code, components, animations, and elegant solutions. But also the human one, made of choices, mistakes, intuitions, and small everyday epiphanies.

I’ll talk about:

- • front-end development, React, Next.js, and everything that excites me about building interfaces
- • design, micro-interactions, aesthetics, and attention to detail
- • workflow, creative processes, and tools that improve the way we work
- • personal reflections on what it means to create in 2026
- • projects, ideas, experiments, and anything worth sharing

It won’t be a “purely technical” blog, nor a personal diary. It will be a bridge between the two.

## A commitment to myself (and to you)

Starting a blog also means making a commitment: to be consistent, to be honest, to be present. I’m not promising a strict schedule, but I am promising authenticity. Every article will be written because I have something to say, not because “I have to publish.”

If you’re here, thank you.  
If you come back, even better.  

## This is just the beginning

Every project begins with a first step. This is mine.  
From today, this blog is open, alive, ready to grow with me and — I hope — with you as well.

Welcome.  
Let’s move forward.
